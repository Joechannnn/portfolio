Designing a gym service that charges by the minute.

The owner wanted to charge HK$1 per minute. As the UX/UI Designer and Product Manager, I defined the product rules, priorities and recovery flows for a 24-hour gym. The work covered the member app, Customer Service Operations and management tools.

The problem

The gym charges HK$1 per minute and has no staff at the door. The product needs to record when members enter and leave and apply a fair cap to longer sessions. It also needs to handle failed OTPs, network requests and access records while protecting member trust and revenue.

To run the gym 24/7, access and minute-based billing needed to work together, with a clear way for Customer Service to help when something went wrong.

What I did

Product rules and MVP priorities

  • Worked with the owner, Customer Service Operations, management and developers to understand daily operations and define the product rules
  • Defined the pricing cap with the owner: HK$1 per minute, capped at a HK$120 Day Pass after two hours
  • Prioritised registration, wallet, access, live minute tracking, checkout and exception recovery for the 2025 MVP; deliberately deferred trainer booking

Member app and login recovery

  • Designed wallet top-up, QR unlock, a manual entry-code fallback and live usage time. Checkout explains the Day Pass cap before charging. For login recovery, members can request one additional WhatsApp OTP resend before contacting Customer Service

Customer Service Operations and exception handling

  • When API or network synchronisation breaks the access record, Customer Service can remotely unlock the door and record the correct entry time. Fee appeals continue the same recovery operation: Customer Service checks CCTV and system logs, accepts or rejects the appeal, corrects the time or fee when justified, and notifies the member

Management across locations

  • Gave management a cross-location view of revenue, usage and member activity. Customer Service handles day-to-day recovery cases separately

Showing members how charges work.

The member screens explain access and pricing before members enter the gym or add money to their wallet.

The outcomes

  • HK$5,000 average monthly incremental revenue generated by the pay-by-minute feature after launch, according to internal business data
  • Launched in 2025 across three Hong Kong locations: Kwai Chung EDGE, Yuen Long and Kwun Tong Eastcore
  • Member, support and management tools launched together. Members complete the normal journey independently, Customer Service handles exceptions, and management monitors the business across locations
