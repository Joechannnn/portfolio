# Joe Chan

Product Design | UX/UI | Project Management

I'm a designer based in Hong Kong. My work covers customer-facing apps and the internal tools that support them, from early requirements through UX/UI, development coordination and launch.

[View portfolio](https://joechannnn.github.io/portfolio/) · [Read CV](https://joechannnn.github.io/portfolio/cv.html)

As UX Designer at Kolorbox Ltd, I lead UX/UI design across BigBigAir's websites, digital services and internal business tools, including edmondpoon.com. Kolorbox provides software development and technical support for its subsidiary BigBigAir. My work covers customer journeys, content, commerce and specialist services. This portfolio also includes work in fintech, fitness and retail.

On 好大個玄機, I manage a portfolio of online service projects for different practitioners. I gather their requirements, design service flows and screens, and coordinate developer handoff and launch.

## Selected work

- [divit Miles Conversion](projects/divit-miles.html): UX for account linking and miles conversion, including work with Cathay Pacific's marketing team on visual branding, copy tone and Asia Miles conversion details.
- [edmondpoon.com](projects/edmondpoon.html): Programme discovery and live participation, with routes into commerce and specialist services.
- [Audience to Action](projects/crm.html): A CRM prototype for audience segmentation and campaign decisions, covering the product model, UX/UI and developer handoff.
- [Inventory in View](projects/inventory.html): Requirements, workflow, GUI design and front-end implementation for stock and replenishment operations.
- [V-FIT24](projects/vfit24.html): UX/UI and project management for Minutes Pay, a pay-per-minute gym service with member, customer-service and management tools. The work covered operational requirements and business rules, requirements review, developer coordination, UAT, marketing promotion and launch.
